small company manufactures customized bicycles , whenever sales dept receives an order a new process instance is created and a member of it can reject or accept order for a customized bike. in the former case instance is finished , in the latter storehouse and engineering dept are informed. storehouse immediatly processes part list of order and checks required quantity of each part , if its available in-house it is reserved otherwise back-ordered , this is repeted for each item on part list. in the meantime engineering dept prepares everything for assembling of ordered bicycle , if storehouse has successfuly reserved or back-ordered every item of list and preparation activity has finished they assemble it. afterwards sales ships bicycle to customer and finishes process instance
