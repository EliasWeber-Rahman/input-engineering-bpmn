after Expense Report is received a new account must be created if employee does not already have one , report is then reviewed for automatic approval. amounts under $200 are automaticaly approved whereas amounts equal to or over $200 require approval of supervisor. in case of rejection employee must receive a rejection notice by email , otherwise reimbursement goes to employees direct deposit bank account. if request is not completed in 7 days he must receive a approval in progress email , if it is not finished within 30 days process is stopped and he receives an email cancellation notice and must re-submit expense report
