The Evanstonian is a upscale independent hotel , when a guest calls room service there room-service manager takes down order and submits order ticket to kitchen to begin preparing food , she also gives a order to sommelier (i.e., the wine waiter) to fetch wine from cellar and prepare any other alcoholic beverages , eighty percent of orders include them. finally she assigns it to waiter , while kitchen and sommelier are doing there tasks he readies a cart (i.e., puts a tablecloth on cart and gathers silverware) and is also responsible for nonalcoholic drinks. once food , wine and cart are ready he delivers it to guest ' s room , after returning to room-service station he debits guest ' s acount but may wait with billing if he has another order to prepare or deliver
