at the beginning customer perceives that her subscribed service has degraded , a list with all problem parameters is sent to Customer Service dept of TELECO where an employee enters (based on received data) a problem report into system T. then it is compared to customer SLA to identify extent and details of service degradation , based on this necessary counter measures are determined including there respective priorities. an electronic service determines significance of customer based on information collected during history of contractual relationship , in case customer is premium process links to an extra problem fix process (not detailed here) , in case customer is of certain significance which would affect counter measures previously decided upon process goes back to re-prioritize them otherwise it continues. taking together information (i.e. contract commitment data + prioritized actions) a detailed problem report is created and sent to Service Management (SM). SM deals on a first level with violations of quality in services provided to customers , after receiving report it investigates wheter problem is analyzable at level of there dept or may be located at Resource Provisioning (RP). if SM assesses it to be not analyzable by themselves report is sent out to RP , if they are sure they can analyze it they perform analysis and based on outcome create a trouble report indicating type of problem. after RP receives report it is checked wheter there are any possible problems , if none are detected a notification about normal service execution is created , if one is detected RP analyzes it and creates a trouble report. either one will be included in a status report and sent back to SM which prepares the final status report based on received information. subsequently it has to be determined what counter measures should be taken depending on info in final status report , three alternative process paths may be taken. if no problem was detected at all actual service performance is sent back to Customer Service. if minor corrective actions are required SM undertakes them by themselves , subsequently problem resolution report is created and sent out to Customer Service and after sending this path of SM ends. if automatic resource restoration from RP is required SM must create a request for it and send it to RP , which has been on-hold and waiting for a restoration request but this must happen within 2 days after status report was sent out otherwise RP terminates the process. after request is received all possible errors are tracked , based on them all necessary corrective actions are undertaken by RP , then a trouble-shooting report is created and sent out to SM ; then process ends. SM receives it and this information goes into creation of problem resolution report just as described for ii). Customer Service either receives actual service performance (if there was no problem) or problem resolution report , then two concurrent activities are triggered , i.e. i) a report is created for customer which details current service performance and resolution of problem , and ii) an SLA violation rebate is reported to Billing & Collections who will adjust billing. report for customer is sent out to her , after all three activities are completed process ends within Customer Service. after customer receives report about service performance and problem resolution from them process flow at customer also ends
