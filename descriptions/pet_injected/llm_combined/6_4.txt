first step is to determine contact details of potential customers , can be achieved in several ways. sometimes we buy details for cold calls , sometimes our marketing staff participates in exhibitions and sometimes you just happen to know somebody who is interested in product. then we start calling them , done by call center staff who are determining contact person and budget which would be available for project. of course asking whether he is generaly interested is also important , if not we leave him alone except if potential project budget is huge , then head of development personaly tries to acquire him. if he is interested in the end next step is a detailed online presentation given either by a sales rep or by a pre-sales employee in case of more technical presentation. afterwards we are waiting for him to come back to us , if we are not contacted within 2 weeks a sales rep is calling him. last phase is creation of a quotation
