As a basic principle ACME AG receives invoices on paper or fax , these are received by Secretariat in central inbox and forwarded after a short visual inspection to an accounting employee. in " ACME Financial Accounting " , a software specially developed for ACME AG , she identifies charging suppliers and creates a new instance (invoice) , then checks invoice items and notes corresponding cost center at ACME AG and related cost center managers for each position on a seperate form (" docket "). docket and copy of invoice go to internal mail together and are sent to first cost center manager to list , he reviews content for accuracy after receiving it. should everything be in order he notes his code one on docket (" accurate position - AP ") and returns copy to internal mail , from it copy is passed on to next cost center manager based on docket or if all items are marked correct sent back to accounting. therefore copy of invoice and docket gradually move through hands of all cost center managers until all positions are marked as completely accurate. however if inconsistencies exist , e.g. because ordered product is not of expected quantity or quality , cost center manager rejects the AP with a note and explanatory statement on docket and copy is sent back to accounting directly. based on statements of managers she will proceede with clarification with vendor but if necessary consults them by telephone or e-mail again. when all inconsistencies are resolved copy is sent to cost center managers again and process continues. after all invoice items are AP accounting employee forwards copy to commercial manager who makes commercial audit and issues approval for payment , if bill amount exceeds EUR 20 Board wants to check it again (4 - eyes-principle). copy of invoice including docket moves back to accounting employee in appropiate signature file. should there be a complaint during commercial audit she resolves it with supplier , after audit is successfully completed she gives payment instructions and closes instance in " ACME financial accounting "
