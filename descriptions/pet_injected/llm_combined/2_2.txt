The process is initiated by a switch-over request , in doing so customer transmits his data to customer service dept of company. customer service is a shared service center between departments Sales and Distribution. data is received by customer service and based on it a customer data object is entered into CRM system , after that it should be compared with internal customer data base and checked for completeness and plausability , in case of errors these should be corrected on basis of a simple error list. comparison is done to prevent individual customer data being stored multiple times. in case customer does not exist in data base a new customer object is being created which will remain the data object of interest during rest of process flow , it consists of data elements such as customer's name and address and assigned power gauge. it is then used in combination with other customer data to prepare contract documents for power supplier switch (including data such as bank connection , information on selected rate , requested date of switch-over). in the following an automated check of contract documents is carried out within CIS (customer information system) in order to confirm there successful generation. in case of negative response , i.e. documents are not (or incorrectly) generated , causing issues are being analyzed and resolved and documents generated once again , in case of positive response a confirmation document is sent out to customer stating that switch-over to new supplier can be executed. a request to grid operator is automatically sent out by CIS asking wheter customer may be supplied by selected supplier in future. grid operator checks it for supplier concurrence and transmits a response comment , in case of supplier concurrence he would inform all involved suppliers and demand resolution of conflict. he communicates with old supplier and carries out termination of sales agreement between customer and old supplier (i.e. customer service (of new supplier) does not have to interact with old supplier regarding termination). if there are not any objections by grid operator (i.e. no supplier concurrence) customer service creates a CIS contract , customer then has chance to check contract details and based on this may decide to either withdraw from switch contract or confirm it. depending on acceptance / rejection process flow at customer service either ends (withdrawal) or continues (confirmation). additional constraint is that customer can only withdraw from offered contract within 7 days after the 7th day contract will be regarded as accepted and process continues , confirmation message by customer is therefore not absolutely neccessary (as it will count as accepted after 7 days anyway) but can speed up switch process. on switch-date , but no later than 10 days after power supply has begun , grid operator transmits power meter data to customer service and old supplier via messages containing a services consumption report. at same time he computes final billing based on meter data and sends it to old supplier , who likewise creates and sends final billing to customer. for customer as well as grid operator process ends then , after receiving meter data customer service imports it to systems that require information. the process of winning a new customer ends here
