an employee purchases a product or service he requires , for instance a sales person on a trip rents a car. he submits an expense report with a list of items along with receipts for each item , a supervisor reviews it and approves or rejects it. since company has expense rules there are circumstances where supervisor can accept or reject upon first inspection , these rules could be automated to reduce workload on supervisor. if he rejects report the employee who submitted it is given a chance to edit it , for example to correct errors or better describe a expense. if he approves it goes to treasurer who checks that all receipts have been submitted and match items on list , if all is in order treasurer accepts expenses for processing (including e.g. payment or refund , and accounting) , if receipts are missing or do not match he sends it back to employee. if a report returns to employee for corrections it must again go to a supervisor , even if he previously approved it. if treasurer accepts expenses for processing report moves to an automatic activity that links to a payment system , process waits for payment confirmation and after it is confirmed process ends
