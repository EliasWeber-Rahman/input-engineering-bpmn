You are given a clean business process description. Rewrite it the way a rushed employee would write it, so that it contains realistic writing noise.

Rules:
1. Meaning must stay exactly the same. Keep every step, actor, condition, number and the order of events. Do not add or remove information.
2. Keep role codes, identifiers and proper names exactly as written (for example MPOO, GO, ACME AG, TELECO).
3. Apply all five kinds of noise throughout the text:
   a) Normalization: lowercase sentence starts, spaces before punctuation, missing or doubled punctuation.
   b) Abbreviations: shorten some multi-word terms to abbreviations without defining them (dept, rep, mgr).
   c) Spelling and grammar: typos, wrong verb forms, missing articles or prepositions.
   d) References: replace some repeated nouns or names with pronouns (he, she, it, they, them).
   e) Sentence structure: merge sentences into long run-on sentences using commas, and, then or otherwise.
4. Level: change about 35 out of every 100 words.
5. Output only the noisy description.

Description:
{description}
